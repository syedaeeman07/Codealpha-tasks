# Phishing Awareness Quiz (10 Easy Questions)

Correct answers are marked with ✅.

**1. What is phishing?**
- A) A game played online
- B) A scam where someone pretends to be trusted to steal your information ✅
- C) A way to make the internet faster

**2. A message says: "You won Rs. 50,000! Click this link to claim." What is it most likely?**
- A) A real prize
- B) A phishing scam ✅

**3. Should you share your OTP code with someone who says they are from your bank?**
- A) Yes, if they sound polite
- B) No, never share your OTP with anyone ✅

**4. Which website address looks fake?**
- A) https://www.facebook.com
- B) https://www.faceb00k-login.com ✅

**5. An email says "Your account will be BLOCKED today! Verify now." What trick is this?**
- A) Creating fear and hurry ✅
- B) Good customer service

**6. What should you do before clicking a link in a message?**
- A) Click it quickly
- B) Check the sender and the link ✅
- C) Forward it to friends

**7. A friend's new number on WhatsApp asks you to send a 6-digit code. What should you do?**
- A) Send the code
- B) Do not send it, the code can unlock your WhatsApp ✅

**8. Does a padlock (🔒) in the address bar mean a website is 100% safe?**
- A) Yes, always
- B) No, fake websites can have a padlock too ✅

**9. What is 2FA (two-factor authentication)?**
- A) An extra security step, like a code, besides your password ✅
- B) A type of virus

**10. You clicked a phishing link by mistake. What should you do?**
- A) Do nothing
- B) Change your password and tell your bank right away ✅
