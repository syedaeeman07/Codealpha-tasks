# Task 2 – Phishing Awareness Training

Task 2 of the **CodeAlpha Cyber Security Internship**.

A short, visual awareness training that teaches people how to recognize and avoid phishing attacks.

## Contents
| File | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `Phishing_Awareness_Training.pdf` | The training presentation (9 slides) |
| `Phishing_Awareness_Training.pptx` | Editable PowerPoint version |
| `Quiz_Questions.md` | 10 quiz questions with answers |

## Topics covered
- What phishing is and how it reaches you (email, SMS/WhatsApp, phone calls)
- Example 1: an annotated fake email
- Example 2: fake SMS and WhatsApp messages
- Example 3: real vs fake website addresses
- 6 warning signs of phishing
- 5 simple rules to stay safe
- Interactive "Phish or real?" quiz

## Key takeaway
**Stop. Think. Verify.** Pause before you click, check the sender and the link, and never share your OTP.
